# little-robot-game
 
A game that a robot echo is stuck on mars, where he needs to find bells that matches melody from machine to unlock the puzzle.
Ekko doesn’t remember
the last time he had fun.
When was the last time
he heard music. His humans
loved to play music with him.
That was long ago, sadly.
Now Ekko strolls through
the world alone.  A machine against time.

He may never play music again, nor will he hear that familiar melody...

Or will he?

Control:
WASD Movement 
E Pickup/Drop/Interact
F Shake the bell 
SPACE Jump
